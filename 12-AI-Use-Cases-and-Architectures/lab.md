# Lab — Module 12: Use Cases and Architectures

## Right service for the right case

Practice quickly mapping between business problem and core AWS service.

## Prerequisites

- Read the module and review AWS AI services.

## Steps

1. List five real use cases: OCR, internal chatbot, image moderation, review sentiment and contract summary.
2. Associate the AWS core service for each.
3. Note whether there would be a complementary service in the architecture.
4. Explain why the chosen option is simpler and more adherent than generic alternatives.
5. Review where Bedrock comes in and where it doesn't need to be the protagonist.

## What to observe

- The best service depends on the dominant verb of the use case.
- Many real solutions use more than one service.

## Costs and cleanup

- Lab with no mandatory cost.
- If you do proofs of concept, turn everything off at the end.
