# Use Cases — Module 06: Amazon SageMaker AI

## Case 1 — Demand forecast

**Scenario:** The company wants to use its own historical data to generate business-specific forecasts.

**Suggested architecture:** ML pipeline with SageMaker AI and datasets versioned in S3.

**Why this choice makes sense:** There is a need for a customized model and lifecycle control.

**Associated exam trap:** Choosing Bedrock just because it is an AI service.

## Case 2 — Business analysts without a mature DS team

**Scenario:** A commercial area wants to quickly test forecasting models without starting with notebooks.

**Suggested architecture:** Canvas as a gateway, with governance and organized data.

**Why this choice makes sense:** The focus here is speed with lower technical barriers.

**Associated exam trap:** Thinking that no-code does not require data preparation.
