# Cheatsheet — Module 01: Introduction to AI and ML

> Quick review designed for the final review of the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam.

## At a glance

| Topic | What to remember | Exam trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI | Broad field that includes ML, computer vision, NLP, and GenAI. | Do not assume that all AI uses LLM. |
| Supervised ML | Train with labeled examples. | Not to be confused with clustering. |
| Inference | Use of the model already trained in production. | Do not treat it as a learning phase. |
| Bedrock | Consumption of managed FMs through an API. | Not to be confused with a complete customized training platform. |

## Practical rules

- Define the success metric before choosing the service.
- Treat training and inference as phases with different costs and risks.
- Prefer managed services when heavy customization is not a requirement.
- Keep data, models and permissions under governance from the start.

## Memory cues

- Training = learning from data; inference = applying the trained model to new inputs.
- Bedrock = use models; SageMaker AI = build and operate ML.
- Generative AI focuses on creation; Classical ML focuses a lot on predicting or classifying.
