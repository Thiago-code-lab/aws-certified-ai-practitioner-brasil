# Flashcards - Introduction to AI and ML

## Card 01

**Question:** What is the difference between AI, ML and deep learning?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
AI is the broad field; ML learns from data; deep learning uses deep neural networks.

</details>

## Card 02

**Question:** What is inference?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Use of the trained model to predict on new data with a focus on latency and cost.

</details>

## Card 03

**Question:** What is training for?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Learn model parameters by optimizing a training objective; use evaluation metrics to assess the result.

</details>

## Card 04

**Question:** When is a problem a classification task?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
When the output is a discrete class.

</details>

## Card 05

**Question:** When should you use regression?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
When the output is a continuous numerical value.

</details>

## Card 06

**Question:** How can you detect overfitting?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Strong training performance with substantially worse validation or test performance.

</details>

## Card 07

**Question:** How can you reduce overfitting?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Use regularization, reduce unnecessary model complexity, and improve the training data.

</details>

## Card 08

**Question:** What is the focus of the exam in this module?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Understand basic concepts to interpret scenarios correctly.

</details>
