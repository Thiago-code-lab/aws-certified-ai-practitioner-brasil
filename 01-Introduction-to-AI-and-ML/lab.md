# Lab — Module 01: Introduction to AI and ML

## Decision map: managed AI services, custom ML, or GenAI

This micro-lab helps train service selection reasoning before getting into specific services.

## Prerequisites

- Have read the module's README.
- Set aside 20 minutes to classify use cases.
- Optional: open the official exam documentation.

## Steps

1. List five real use cases from your company or context: prediction, classification, OCR, chatbot, internal search.
2. For each case, answer: is the expected result to predict, classify, retrieve or generate content?
3. Select which option suits you best: ready-made service, customized ML or foundation model.
4. Review the reasons and identify where Bedrock or SageMaker AI would appear.
5. Compare your answer with the module tips and adjust your reasoning.

## What to observe

- Not every AI use case needs a custom model.
- The desired result defines the architectural path.
- The exam question often hides the problem category in a business description.

## Costs and cleanup

- There is no mandatory cost for this lab if you are just practicing architecture.
- If you open services on AWS, don't leave persistent resources unnecessarily.
