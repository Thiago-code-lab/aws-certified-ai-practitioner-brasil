# Cheatsheet — Module 07: Responsible AI and Governance

> Quick review designed for the final review of the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam.

## At a glance

| Topic | What to remember | Exam trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A2I | Human review at critical points. | Do not skip it when the scenario requires human review. |
| Clarify | Bias and explainability in ML. | Not to be confused with generation service. |
| Guardrails | Content policies for GenAI. | They do not replace access control. |
| CloudTrail | Audit trail and traceability. | Without logs, governance is weak. |

## Practical rules

- Use human review in high-impact scenarios.
- Set explicit policies for acceptable data, prompts, and responses.
- Monitor deviant behavior, security and user complaints.
- Evaluate bias and explainability when there is a sensitive decision.

## Memory cues

- High risk -> human review.
- Governance = policy + logs + process + accountability.
- Responsible AI does not live in isolation from the rest of the architecture.
