# Lab — Module 07: Responsible AI and Governance

## Responsible AI checklist for a sensitive case

Apply responsible AI principles to a realistic exam scenario.

## Prerequisites

- Read the entire module.
- Choose a high-impact case: credit, HR, health or compliance.

## Steps

1. Describe the case and identify the impact on the end user.
2. List which decisions can be automated and which require humans.
3. Define what logs, approvals and policies the application must have.
4. Choose where Guardrails, A2I and CloudTrail would help.
5. Review whether the architecture is auditable and explainable.

## What to observe

- Responsible AI is most convincing when translated into concrete controls.
- The exam values balance between productivity and user protection.

## Costs and cleanup

- Conceptual lab, with no mandatory cost.
- If you run a real proof of concept, do not use personal data without authorization.
