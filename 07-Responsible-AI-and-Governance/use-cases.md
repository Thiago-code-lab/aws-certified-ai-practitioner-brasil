# Use Cases — Module 07: Responsible AI and Governance

## Case 1 — CV screening

**Scenario:** An HR team wants to use AI to rank resumes, but needs to avoid bias and maintain human decision-making.

**Suggested architecture:** Pipeline with transparent criteria, mandatory human review and periodic bias assessment.

**Why this choice makes sense:** AI supports productivity, but it should not concentrate decision-making without oversight.

**Associated exam trap:** Transform ranking into final automatic decision.

## Case 2 — Financial service bot

**Scenario:** The company wants to block responses that are inappropriate advice.

**Suggested architecture:** Guardrails, subject policies and human escalation when necessary.

**Why this choice makes sense:** Responsible AI is also about limiting the system's scope of action.

**Associated exam trap:** Allowing the model to answer anything in the name of agility.
