# Flashcards - Responsible AI and Governance

## Card 01

**Question:** What is bias?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

## Card 02

**Question:** Fairness differs from accuracy how?

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

**Answer:**
Fairness assesses equitable outcomes; accuracy measures overall predictive correctness.

</details>

<details>
