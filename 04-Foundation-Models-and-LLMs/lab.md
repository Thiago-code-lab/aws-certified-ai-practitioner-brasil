# Lab — Module 04: Foundation Models and LLMs

## Designing a RAG flow in AWS

Visually consolidate the chain between data, embeddings, retrieval and generation.

## Prerequisites

- Have read modules 01 to 04.
- Know the basic concepts of S3 and Bedrock.

## Steps

1. List a small set of documents that would make sense as a knowledge base.
2. Define how these documents would be stored and versioned in S3.
3. Describe how the text would be divided into chunks and converted into embeddings.
4. Choose where the vectors would be stored and how the retrieval would occur.
5. Explain how LLM would receive the question and retrieved context.

## What to observe

- RAG has two distinct stages: retrieval and generation.
- Without document curation, LLM remains vulnerable to bad context.

## Costs and cleanup

- Lab can be just architectural, free of charge.
- If you test services, remove indexes and temporary bases after use.
