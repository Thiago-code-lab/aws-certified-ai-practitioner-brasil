# Use Cases — Module 13: AWS AI Services

## Case 1 — Recommendation in e-commerce

**Scenario:** The company wants to personalize suggestions without building a deep ML team.

**Suggested architecture:** Amazon Personalize.

**Why this choice makes sense:** It's the expert, managed answer to personalized recommendation.

**Associated exam trap:** Choosing an LLM just because 'generative AI is in'.

## Case 2 — Assisted corporate search

**Scenario:** Employees need to find answers in AI-powered business content.

**Suggested architecture:** Amazon Q Business or case-oriented Bedrock architecture, depending on the context.

**Why this choice makes sense:** The dominant requirement is productivity and guided business search.

**Associated exam trap:** Responding only with 'chatbot' without thinking about the product context.
