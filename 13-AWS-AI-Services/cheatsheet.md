# Cheatsheet — Module 13: AWS AI Services

> Quick review designed for the final review of the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam.

## At a glance

| Topic | What to remember | Exam trap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comprehend | Managed NLP for sentiment, entity and classification. | Not to be confused with open generation. |
| Textract | Documents and forms. | Do not use it as if it were a recommendation service. |
| Personalize | Personalized recommendations. | It's not a business search. |
| Amazon Q Business | Search and assisted productivity for companies. | It's not just a generic chatbot. |

## Practical rules

- Learn services by business problem, not alphabetically.
- Differentiate specialized services from ML and GenAI platforms.
- Prioritize the official list of services within the scope of the exam.
- Understand the conceptual limits of each service.

## Memory cues

- Speech, document, vision, recommendation and GenAI are different families.
- Specialized service first; LLM when open language is really the value.
