# Lab — Module 02: Fundamentals of Generative AI

## Comparing prompts and response variation

Explore how context, format, and temperature change the behavior of a generative application.

## Prerequisites

- AWS account with access to Bedrock or conceptual exercise only.
- Know the basic concepts of the module.

## Steps

1. Create a simple prompt asking for a corporate policy summary.
2. Redo the prompt stating role, objective, word limit and output format.
3. Compare the quality of the answer with and without document context.
4. Test at low and then moderate temperatures, recording differences.
5. Note which combination was best for the operational task.

## What to observe

- A more structured prompt usually generates a more predictable response.
- Temperature affects style and variability.
- External context reduces undue improvisation.

## Costs and cleanup

- Avoid testing with sensitive documents without an approved policy.
- If you use resources in the account, close everything at the end.
