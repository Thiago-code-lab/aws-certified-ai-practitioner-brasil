# Use Cases — Module 02: Generative AI Fundamentals

## Case 1 — HR knowledge base

**Scenario:** The HR department wants to answer questions about benefits without inventing policies that don't exist.

**Suggested architecture:** Application with Bedrock and grounding mechanism on approved internal documents.

**Why this choice makes sense:** The value is in combining generation with a trusted corporate context.

**Associated exam trap:** Relying only on pre-trained knowledge of the model.

## Case 2 — Marketing campaigns

**Scenario:** The marketing team wants to generate slogan variations for A/B testing.

**Suggested architecture:** Prompt engineering and higher temperature may be useful as long as human review validates brand adherence.

**Why this choice makes sense:** Here, diversity is more relevant than absolute rigidity.

**Associated exam trap:** Apply the same configuration used in regulated processes.
