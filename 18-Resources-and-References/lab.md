# Lab — Resources and Links

## Building your final review plan

1. Open the official exam guide and read the domains.
2. List the modules in the repository that you have the most difficulty with.
3. Associate an official resource for each difficulty: Bedrock, SageMaker AI, security, or practice.
4. Create a 3-day plan with reading + questions + short review.
5. Finish with a practice exam and the glossary.
