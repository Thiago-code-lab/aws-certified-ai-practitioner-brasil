## Final review plan

### 7 days before the exam

- Review modules 01 to 05 to reinforce fundamentals, GenAI and Bedrock.
- Review modules 07, 11 and 15 for responsible AI, security and cost.
- Complete the first practice exam and fill in the error log.

### 3 days before the exam

- Redo only wrong questions.
- Read the [Glossary](../17-Glossary/README.md).
- Review cheatsheets for weaker modules.

### 24 hours before the exam

- Read only short reviews, without opening new topics.
- Review classic traps: Bedrock vs SageMaker AI, RAG vs fine-tuning, Guardrails vs IAM.
- Check exam logistics and rest.
