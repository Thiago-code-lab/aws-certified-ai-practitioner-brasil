## Official format of the AIF-C01 exam

- 65 questions in total.
- 50 scored questions.
- 15 unscored questions.
- 90 minutes.
- Scaled score from 100 to 1,000; minimum passing score 700.
- No penalty for guessing.
- The official guide permits these types: multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, and matching.

## Planning a 65-question practice session

The source suggested combining these files into a 65-question session, but the actual bank has only 30 occurrences, including eight duplicates. It cannot provide a full-length 65-question mock exam. Use these exercises for targeted practice and consult official AWS preparation resources for additional questions and formats.

### Recommended configuration for this session

- Reserve **90 minutes** for a separate full-length 65-question practice test.
- Do not consult material in the middle of the simulation.
- Mark doubtful questions and move on.
- Only open the explanations after finishing.
- Record errors in [`error-log.md`](./error-log.md).

## Recommended study strategy

1. Complete the [`questions.md`](./questions.md) block to warm up vocabulary, question types and context reading.
2. Solve [`full-practice-exam-1.md`](./full-practice-exam-1.md) with a time limit suitable for eight questions.
3. Complete the [`error-log.md`](./error-log.md) before reviewing the answer explanations.
4. Use [`domain-practice-exams.md`](./domain-practice-exams.md) to identify gaps, then revisit the corresponding lessons.
5. Close with [`full-practice-exam-2.md`](./full-practice-exam-2.md), [`cheatsheet.md`](./cheatsheet.md) and [`final-review-aif-c01.md`](./final-review-aif-c01.md).

## Practical readiness ranges

> These ranges are **study heuristics**, not official equivalents of AWS scaled scores. A small bank with repeated questions cannot establish exam readiness by itself.

| Raw practice result | Study interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 85% or more | Strong performance on this practice bank; confirm understanding with unfamiliar questions |
| 75% to 84% | Good base; still worth reviewing right service for right case and governance pitfalls |
| 65% to 74% | Risk zone; return to modules 04, 05, 07, 11, 13 and 15 |
| Below 65% | It's not time to rely solely on simulations; review the concepts and then repeat the training |

## Final tips

- Read the entire use case before looking at the alternatives.
- Prioritize the best-managed option when two alternatives seem equivalent.
- Clearly differentiate Bedrock, SageMaker AI, and specialized AI services.
- When the question mentions security or governance, look for IAM, KMS, CloudTrail, Secrets Manager, Macie, Guardrails, and human review.
- Review the official **services in scope** page before the exam, as AWS may update this list over time.
