# Cheatsheet — Practice Exams and Questions

## Exam format

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Total questions | 65 |
| Scored questions | 50 |
| Unscored questions | 15 |
| Total time | 90 minutes |
| Minimum score | 700/1000 |
| Question types | multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, and matching |

## Quick reading cues

| Situation | How to act | Exam clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confusion between Bedrock and SageMaker AI | Return to modules 05 and 06 | Does the question talk about consuming FM or building ML? |
| Error in RAG | Review modules 04 and 08 | Does the question mention updated internal documents? |
| Error in Responsible AI | Review modules 07 and 11 | Is there sensitive decision making, bias, auditing or human review? |
| Error in cost | Review module 15 | Does the company need operational efficiency or lower expenses? |
| Security error | Review module 11 | Does the question talk about access, secrecy, encryption, PII or traceability? |

## Response Heuristics

- Specialized service usually beats broad LLM when the problem is objective.
- Bedrock usually wins over its own infrastructure when the requirement is to consume FM with less operation.
- SageMaker AI usually beats Bedrock when the question asks for training, deep customization or ML lifecycle.
- Guardrails do not replace IAM, KMS, logs or human review.
- RAG is not synonymous with fine-tuning.

## Frequent pitfalls

- Choose GenAI when the question asks for OCR, recommendation or sentiment.
- Treat embeddings as if they generated a final response.
- Ignore cost of context and output size.
- Thinking that a single content policy solves entire governance.
