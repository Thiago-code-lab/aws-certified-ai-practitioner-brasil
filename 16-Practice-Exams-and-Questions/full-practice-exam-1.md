# Full Practice Exam 1 — Curated Edition

## Question 01

**Question:** When the statement calls for "lower operational effort" for GenAI, which direction is usually more appropriate?

- A) Managed service
- B) Complete self-managed infrastructure
- C) Own distributed training
- D) Customized MLOps pipeline from scratch

<details>
<summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

✅ **Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:**
At the AI Practitioner level, "lower operational effort" typically points to managed services.

**Why option B is wrong:**
Increases operational complexity without need in the scenario described.

</details>

## Question 02

**Question:** Embeddings are mainly used for:

- A) Generate audio
- B) Semantic retrieval
- C) Generate DNS
- D) Encrypt buckets

<details><summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

✅ **Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:**
Embeddings convert text into vectors to search for semantic similarity.

**Why option A is wrong:**
Audio generation is a case of speech services, not embeddings.

</details>

## Question 04

**Question:** Which control is most aligned to the audit trail of actions?

- A) IAM
- B) CloudTrail
- C) Route 53
- D) WAF

<details><summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

✅ **Correct answer:** B

**Explanation:**
CloudTrail records API events and actions for auditing and compliance.

**Why option A is wrong:**
IAM defines permission; Event auditing is another function.

</details>

## Question 05

**Question:** To limit inappropriate output from a Bedrock assistant, you must prioritize:

- A) Guardrails
- B) NAT Gateway
- C) EBS
- D) ELB

<details><summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

✅ **Correct answer:** A

## Question 08

**Question:** In case of use with low latency and controlled cost, the recommended initial practice is:

- A) Start with a smaller model and measure quality
- B) Always use the largest model available
- C) Ignore observability
- D) Increase temperature for everything

<details><summary><strong>See answer</strong></summary>

✅ **Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:**
Progressive model choice reduces cost and latency without sacrificing quality when validated by metrics.

**Why option B is wrong:**
Larger model increases cost/latency with no guarantee of real benefit.

</details>
