## Exam time strategy

- **First pass:** easy and medium questions.
- **Second pass:** multiple responses, ordering and association.
- **Third pass:** items marked with greater doubt.

### Pacing practice goal

- Up to 30 minutes: be close to question 22.
- Up to 60 minutes: be close to question 44.
- Last 30 minutes: complete the remaining questions, then review flagged items.

## What not to forget

- The exam does not require detailed ML pipeline implementation or advanced mathematics.
- The exam favors reading the use case and choosing the most appropriate, managed and safe option.
- When in doubt between specialized service and broad LLM, ask first:
  - is the problem already well resolved by a specific service?
  - is there a real requirement for open language generation?

## Final checklist

- I know the difference between Bedrock, SageMaker AI and specialized services.
- I can explain RAG, embeddings and grounding without confusing them with fine-tuning.
- I know how to recognize when human review is mandatory.
- I know how to identify the minimum controls for IAM, KMS, logs and secrets.
- I know the official exam format and have practiced a comparable question volume using additional official preparation resources; this repository alone has only 30 multiple-choice occurrences.
